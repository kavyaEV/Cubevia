Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information Cubevia Ltd collects through this website, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

Cubevia Ltd is the data controller for the information described here. We are a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 17352353, with a registered office at 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NA, United Kingdom.

For anything in this notice, contact us at contact@cubevia.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into the enquiry form on this site:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • Your company name, if you give it
  • Your phone number, if you give it
  • The service and number of tenders per month you select
  • Anything you write in the message field

We do not run analytics, advertising trackers or any third-party scripts on this site, and we do not set cookies. The animation code and fonts are served from our own site. Nothing about your visit is recorded unless you submit the form.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use these details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and discuss whether our services fit your operation. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to a business enquiry you chose to send us. If we go on to work together, we will process your details to perform our contract with you.

We will not add you to a marketing list from a form submission, and we will not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form delivery service that passes the message on by email. Our website is hosted by Cloudflare, which serves the page files. Our email is handled by our email provider. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and may store or transmit data outside the UK, protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

We share your information with no one else unless we are legally required to.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 24 months. If we become a supplier to you, we keep records for as long as the relationship lasts and then for six years afterwards, to meet UK accounting and tax requirements.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Transfer it to another provider

Email contact@cubevia.com and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, so anything you submit is encrypted in transit. Access to enquiries is limited to people at Cubevia who need it to reply to you.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and change the date at the top.
