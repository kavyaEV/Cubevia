AI built around your pre-construction workflow

We set up AI around the way your team already works, then run it alongside you. It reads, sorts and drafts. Your estimators make the decisions.

  • A person checks every output. Nothing reaches your client without review by a Cubevia estimator or your own team.
  • Set up around your tools. Outlook, Gmail, SharePoint, Excel and the tender portals you already use.
  • Confidential by default. Drawings and pricing are used only for your work, and we're happy to sign your NDA.

  1. Enquiry intake

    Reads incoming emails and portal notices, then logs each invitation to tender with client, package, deadline and documents.

  2. Scope check

    Reviews drawings and specifications against your scope and flags gaps, exclusions and compliance requirements.

  3. Organise

    Keeps the tender register current, files documents and reminds you before every return date.

  4. Take-off assist

    Pulls quantities and schedules into your own pricing template, ready for an estimator to check.

  5. Client replies

    Drafts clarifications, acknowledgements and follow-ups for you to approve and send.
